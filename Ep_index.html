Our mission

To inspire people to donate their unused stationery and educational supplies to enhance the quality of education of children in need.

Our vision

A world in which every child attains the right to have educational supplies in order to receive a quality education.

Our Story

Earthed Project was founded in Madrid in 2015 by Stacey and Dominique (Nixee).

While travelling, Stacey and Nixee noticed that in many parts of the world, children attend class without essential supplies. Notebooks, pens, pencils and other simple tools that are crucial for learning.

They saw a chance not only to raise awareness, but to build a platform where individuals and organisations can help by donating stationery.

Stationery nurtures a child's ability to write, solve problems and express creativity, and ultimately to grow intellectually. By providing these tools, Earthed Project helps level the playing field and opens up more opportunities for academic success.

How it works

Cycle trip

Each year we organise a cycling journey that takes participants across different parts of the world.

Collaborations

Through different social platforms and FundRazr, we ask for donations from people and companies.

Donations

With generous contributions from the public, we receive donations for children, schools, and orphanages in need.

Our latest trip: Florence to Zagreb

FlorenceZagreb

In October 2025, five of us cycled 723 km (450 miles) from Florence, Italy to Zagreb, Croatia, collecting funds and school supplies for children who go without.

The money and supplies went to Dr. Kadi A. Sesay Government Girls Secondary School in Port Loko, Sierra Leone. It opened in 2020 as the first modern girls' secondary school in its region, and has 501 students and 25 teachers. Even so, it struggles to get basic supplies to every student.
